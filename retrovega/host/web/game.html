RetroVega

The game compiled for your browser · build @REV@

Flight deck

The mouse is the pointer: click to walk the base, and in flight it steers. Space fires, Tab moves between the things you can use, the arrows steer the pointer. Caps Lock is the game's Esc, because the browser keeps Esc for leaving full screen. Your bunk's saves stay in this browser. On a touch screen a tap is a click, and Keys (your device's keyboard), Esc and Fire are under the picture, or in its corners in full screen.

This is RetroVega itself, built for the web: no emulator. To fly it with other pilots, use the DOSBox page (the multiplayer layer lives there); the game's logic alone runs in rvsim.
