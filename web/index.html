        Controller buttons and axes
        

        

        

          
          
          
          
          
        

        
The controller is the game's mouse and keyboard: the sticks steer (left stick turns and pitches, right stick pitches and rolls), the buttons press the game's keys. In menus and the barracks the left stick moves the pointer and the Fire guns button clicks. Each browser window has its own controller, so two windows side by side make a split screen: one controller each, or a controller in one and the keyboard and mouse in the other.
